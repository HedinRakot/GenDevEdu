Text operations include string concatenation.

Example:

Let's assume you are writing a website. On the homepage, you need to display text from two different sources. What would you write?

```csharp
string text = "The child went to kindergarten on the first day";
var illness = " and got sick";
var combinedText = text + illness;
```

The value of the variable "combinedText" will be the text: "The child went to kindergarten on the first day and got sick", i.e., the two texts are combined into one.

The results of text concatenation will not be displayed separately in the program, but they will be stored in the computer's virtual memory. We will discuss how to display them on the screen later.
