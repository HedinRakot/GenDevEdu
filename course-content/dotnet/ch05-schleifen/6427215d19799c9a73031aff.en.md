Suppose we need to conduct a survey for the age group from 15 to 21 years.

**Example.**

```csharp
var age = Console.ReadLine();
if (age >= 15 && age <= 21)
{
}
```

In this example we check that the age of the survey participant is greater than 15 years and less than 21 years. To do this, we used the special symbols **&&**. They mean that both conditions must be met at the same time.

Now let's conduct a survey for the age group under 18 years and over 45 years.

**Example.**

```csharp
var age = Console.ReadLine();
if (age < 18 || age > 45)
{
}
```

In this example we check that the age of the survey participant is either under 18 years or over 45 years. To do this, we used the special symbols **||**. They mean that either the first condition or the second condition must be met.
