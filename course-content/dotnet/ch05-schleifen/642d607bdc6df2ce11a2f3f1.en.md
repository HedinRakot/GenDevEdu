**Exercise 1.**

Calculate the sum of the numbers from 0 to 100. 0 + 1 + 2 + ... + 100 and print the result to the screen.

**Exercise 2.**

Print the text line by line.

```
Line 1.
Line 2.
Line 3.
 ...
Line 99.
```

**Exercise 3.**

Print the numbers from 10 to 20 and from 40 to 50.

**Exercise 4.**

Calculate the sum of the numbers from 0 to 1000. When the sum exceeds 100,000, print the last number that was added.

**Exercise 5.**

Print all even numbers from 0 to 100 on the screen.

Tip: a number is even when the remainder of dividing this number by 2 equals 0.
